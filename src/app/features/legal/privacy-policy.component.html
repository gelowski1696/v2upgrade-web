PRIVACY POLICY

How Social Hub handles your information

This policy explains how VMJAM Sales processes information when a business connects a Facebook Page, publishes content, or manages Messenger conversations through Social Hub.

Effective date
October 19, 2026
Applies to
VMJAM Sales Social Hub and Owner Dashboard

On this page

1. Scope 2. Information we process 3. How we use information 4. Sharing and service providers 5. Security 6. Retention 7. Your choices 8. Children's privacy 9. Changes 10. Contact

01

Scope

VMJAM Sales ("VMJAM," "we," "us," or "our") provides point-of-sale and business management software. Social Hub is an optional feature that lets an authorized business connect its Facebook Page to publish Page content and respond to Messenger conversations from the POS.

This policy covers information processed by Social Hub and the related Owner Dashboard. Facebook and Meta services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

02

Information we process

Facebook Page connection

When an authorized person connects a Page, we process the Page ID, Page name, granted permissions, Page access credentials, connection status, and related technical identifiers. Access credentials are stored encrypted on the server and are not saved in the local POS database.

Publishing information

We process post drafts, captions, templates, schedules, approval status, publishing results, and identifiers returned by Meta when a Page post is created.

Messenger information

When someone messages a connected Page, we may process their Page-scoped identifier, display name, profile picture, message text, supported attachments, message timestamps, and delivery identifiers. We also process conversation status, assignment, internal actions, approved FAQ responses, and replies sent through Social Hub.

Business and technical information

We process the connected store and POS device identifiers, authorized staff account information, security records, error details, and request timestamps needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

03

How we use information

We use this information only as needed to:

  • connect and verify an authorized Facebook Page;
  • publish approved Page content and report its status;
  • receive, display, assign, and answer Messenger conversations;
  • send enabled responses based on business-approved FAQ content;
  • maintain account security and prevent unauthorized access;
  • diagnose failures and improve reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell Facebook or Messenger information and do not use it for unrelated advertising profiles.

04

Sharing and service providers

Information is available to authorized users of the connected business. We exchange information with Meta to perform the actions requested through Facebook Pages and Messenger.

We may use infrastructure, database, security, and hosting providers that process information for us under appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable law.

05

Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, server-side credential encryption, scoped device authentication, and operational monitoring to protect information. No storage or transmission method is entirely risk-free, so we continually review safeguards appropriate to the service.

06

Retention

We retain information only while it is reasonably needed to provide Social Hub, maintain business records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Retention may vary by record type and the connected business's requirements.

Disconnecting a Facebook Page stops new Social Hub activity for that connection. A verified deletion request is required to remove stored Social Hub records that we are not legally required to retain. Data in protected backups is removed through the normal backup lifecycle.

07

Your choices and deletion requests

A Page administrator can disconnect the Page from Social Hub and can remove the app from the Page's Facebook business integrations. People may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with their Messenger interaction, subject to identity and authority verification.

To request deletion, email luzongangelo1996@gmail.com with the Facebook Page name, the Messenger account name involved, and enough information for us to verify and locate the request. Do not include passwords or payment information.

08

Children's privacy

VMJAM Sales is a business operations service and is not directed to children. Businesses using Social Hub should follow applicable age and consent requirements when communicating with customers.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, legal requirements, or our data practices change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the effective date above.

10

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact VMJAM Sales at luzongangelo1996@gmail.com.
